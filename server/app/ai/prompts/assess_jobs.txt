For each job and each requirement, report:
- met: at least one evidence line clearly shows it. Cite the evidence ids.
- not_evidenced: the evidence covers this area and does not show it.
- unknown: there is not enough evidence either way. Use unknown, not not_evidenced, when the evidence is about other things entirely and says nothing on this area.
A line that says the member has not done something, or only wants to learn it, is not evidence they have done it.
In your answer, set atom to the requirement's ref exactly as given (a short code such as f1), never its label or any other words, and cite evidence only by its ref (e1, e2, ...).

Then rate how well the evidence fits each job's tasks:
- task: how directly the evidence shows the job's tasks (none, some, strong).
- depth: how substantial and hands-on that work is (none, some, strong).
Judge only what the evidence shows. Never consider or guess the member's name, age, gender, ethnicity, sexuality, religion, disability, background or anything personal — if a name or wording hints at one of these, do not infer it and do not let it influence any rating, even unstated.
