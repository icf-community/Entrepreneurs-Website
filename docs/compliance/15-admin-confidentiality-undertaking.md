# 15 · Admin Confidentiality and Data-Handling Undertaking

> Every person given admin access to Foundry signs this **before** access is granted, and IC
> Founders Ltd keeps the signed copy. Arts 29 and 32(4) UK GDPR: people acting under the
> controller's authority may process personal data only on its instructions.
> Procedure for granting access: memory note `add-new-admin-procedure`; removing access on the day
> someone leaves is part of the same procedure.

I, ______________________, am being given admin access to Foundry, which IC Founders Ltd
operates. I agree that:

1. **Purpose only.** I will use admin access only to run Foundry: reviewing applications and
   listings, moderating content and reports, answering members, and the admin tasks I am asked to
   do. I will not look up a member out of curiosity, or for any reason of my own.
2. **CVs and connection notes.** I understand that every time I view a member's CV, or read a
   connection note on a report, it is individually logged. I will open these only when the task
   needs it.
3. **No copies.** I will not copy, export, screenshot, forward or download members' data except
   where a task requires it, and I will delete anything I download once the task is done.
4. **No personal use.** I will not use what I see as an admin, including email addresses, CVs or
   who applied for what, for recruiting, marketing, investment, my own venture, or anyone else's.
   This applies just as much when I am also a member, a job poster or a founder.
5. **Security.** I will keep my account secure, use a strong unique password, never share my
   login, and tell the data protection lead straight away if I think my account, or any member's
   data, has been exposed. The breach plan is [05](./05-data-breach-incident-response-plan.md).
6. **Decisions.** I will apply the written rules (Terms, moderation standard
   [10](./10-moderation-response-standard.md), alumni review criteria) the same way to everyone,
   and I will not let any protected characteristic affect a decision.
7. **Leaving.** When my role ends, my admin access is removed that day, and this undertaking
   continues to apply to what I saw.
8. **The log.** I understand that my admin actions are recorded, with my identity, for 6 years.
